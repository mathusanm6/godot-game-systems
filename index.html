Card system

Playable in the browser

A deckbuilder-style card system built on a standard 52-card deck. Cards fan out in a curved hand and can be dropped into slots on the table.

  • Curved hand with fanned card rotation
  • Draw pile and discard pile
  • Slot docking, swapping and unslotting
  • Drag to reorder the hand, up to 8 cards
